Click a notice or press F2 for its full text. In details: arrows/Page Up/Down scroll, C copies the sample message, Esc returns. During review: O reopens, C copies the link, Esc twice requests cancellation. These actions are simulated. The timeout is held for inspection unless enabled above. The draft is a fixture.






Keep the notice small
One rounded notice, using the existing success, warning, and error colors. Longer messages wrap; their full text stays available in a read-only view.


Protect the active input
Notices stay inside the conversation area. They wait behind pickers and never push the chat bar. Opening details does not discard the draft or stop work.


Keep the status honest
The green review wave stays after context. Ready does not mean finished. Returned feedback goes into the draft for you to send; an error does not erase it.
